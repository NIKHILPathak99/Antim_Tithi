Hamara Parichay

Formed so that no family has to arrange a funeral alone.

Antimtithi began in Varanasi in 2016, after our founders watched a family spend the night of a bereavement negotiating with wood sellers. We built the service we wished they had — one call, one price, every arrangement handled.

We are coordinators, not merely vendors. When a family calls, one person stays with them from the first hour through the thirteenth day — arranging the freezer box and hearse, completing municipal documentation, engaging the pandit, sourcing samagri and booking the ghat.

Our pandit network is drawn from Kashi, Gaya, Haridwar and Prayagraj, with each priest verified for lineage and paddhati. Rituals are explained in plain Hindi or English as they happen, so the family understands what is being offered and why.

We serve families of every background and never place a condition of caste, community or means on the help we give. Where a family cannot pay, our trust fund covers the cremation cost in full.

How we work

Shastra first

Every rite follows the paddhati of your family's tradition. If a ritual does not apply to you, we say so rather than sell it.

One fixed price

Quotes include pandit dakshina, samagri and logistics. Nothing extra is asked at the ghat, ever.

Present in an hour

A coordinator reaches the family within 60–90 minutes in our core cities, day or night.

Quiet conduct

Our staff do not photograph families, do not upsell, and never discuss one family's rites with another.
